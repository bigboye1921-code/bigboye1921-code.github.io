What is Bitcoin?

Bitcoin is a decentralized digital currency that uses a public blockchain to record transactions without a traditional central bank.

What is a crypto wallet?

A wallet helps you manage the keys used to access digital assets. Learn why protecting recovery phrases is essential.

How blockchain works

A blockchain is a shared ledger where transactions are recorded in linked blocks and verified by a network.

Security basics

Protect your recovery phrase

Never share a recovery phrase or private key with another person. Store backups securely and offline when appropriate.

Watch for scams

Be cautious of guaranteed-profit promises, fake giveaways, impersonation and requests to send crypto to unlock a reward.

Verify before sending

Check wallet addresses and network details carefully. Crypto transactions can be difficult or impossible to reverse.
